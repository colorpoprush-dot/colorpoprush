Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Vexora Labs handles information in Color Pop Rush, including the Android game and its browser version. For privacy questions, contact colorpoprush@gmail.com.

Game progress on your device

The game stores scores, coins, unlocked themes, achievements, missions, daily rewards, settings and unfinished rounds in local storage on your device or browser. These records allow the game to resume and remember your progress. The current version does not provide player accounts or automatic cloud synchronization, and Vexora Labs does not receive your saved gameplay records through a game server.

If you use Export backup, the game creates a file containing your progress. You control where you save or share it. Restore backup reads a file you choose and replaces the saved progress on that device.

Advertising and privacy choices

The Android app integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage applicable privacy choices. The browser game does not display native AdMob ads. Development builds may use test ads.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or account identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Collection depends on SDK behavior, device settings and applicable consent choices. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit.

The current game requests non-personalized ads. Non-personalized ads can still involve device information and storage; they do not mean that no data is processed. Where required, a consent message is shown before ads can be requested. You can revisit available choices through Settings → Ad privacy choices. Android's advertising privacy settings also provide controls for your advertising ID.

For Google's practices and controls, see Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

Browser hosting

The browser version and this policy are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses and request logs, when serving these pages. See GitHub's privacy statement. The game itself does not include a separate analytics service.

Support and sharing

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond, troubleshoot and handle privacy requests. Avoid including passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. We do not sell your support messages or local game saves. We may disclose support information when required by law or needed to protect users and the service.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you clear the app's storage or the browser's site data; clearing it removes progress. Exported backups remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately. Device-level backups may also be controlled by your operating system or backup provider.

We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve your request and meet any applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us. Google and GitHub control retention of information they process under their policies; clearing game storage does not delete their records.

Children and privacy rights

The game does not ask players to submit their name, date of birth or contact details. If you believe a child has sent personal information to Vexora Labs, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate. Rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information, or to withdraw consent, depend on applicable law. Contact us for requests involving information we hold.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when game features or data practices change. We will publish revisions here and update the effective date. Significant changes may also be communicated in the game where appropriate.
